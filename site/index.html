Chrome extension for Google Sheets

Google Sheets, finally dark. Red still means over budget.

Nightcell repaints the grid as Sheets draws it. Pastel rows become deep tints, red totals stay red, and all 180 fill and text pairs we test read at 5:1 contrast or better, in every theme. Menus, sidebars and dialogs go dark too.

Why inverting breaks a spreadsheet

Invert-style dark modes flip every colour to its opposite, so a red total turns cyan. Nightcell keeps each hue and moves it into the dark.

Your call, per sheet

Press Alt Shift D to switch without reloading. Keep one sheet light while the rest go dark, or follow your computer's appearance.

Nothing else changes

Your file is untouched. Collaborators see the normal sheet, colour pickers show the real colours, and printing or exporting a PDF stays light.

No servers. No analytics. One permission.

Nightcell makes no network requests and never reads, stores or sends what is in your cells. The only permission is storage, for your theme. Read the privacy policy or check the code.

Questions people ask first

Does it change my spreadsheet?

No. It only changes how the page is drawn on your screen. The file, your collaborators' view, print and PDF export all stay exactly as they were.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Nightcell is open source under the MIT licence.

Can it read my data?

It swaps colours as Sheets draws them and never looks at the text or numbers. It has no server to send anything to, and it makes no network requests at all.

Will it break when Google updates Sheets?

The grid engine works at the drawing level and does not depend on Google's class names. For menus and dialogs, a safety net darkens any light pop-up it finds, so a new Google panel is dark on day one, and fixes ship on GitHub.

Does it work on Google Docs or Slides?

Not yet. It is built for Sheets, where cell colours carry meaning and a simple invert does the most damage.
