Invert-style dark modes turn your red total cyan

Most dark-mode extensions flip every colour to its opposite, so meaning flips with it. Nightcell keeps each hue and moves it into the dark.

180 colour pairs, every one at 5:1 or better

The test suite pairs 20 common cell fills with 9 text colours and fails the build if any pair drops below 5:1 in any theme. WCAG AA asks for 4.5:1. Below, all 180 as Nightcell draws them in Graphite.

Questions people ask before installing

What does it cost?

Nothing. Nightcell is free and open source under the MIT licence, with no account and no paid tier.

Does it change my spreadsheet?

No. It only changes how the page is drawn on your screen. The file, your collaborators' view, print and PDF export stay exactly as they were.

Can it read my data?

It swaps colours and fonts as Sheets draws them and never looks at the text or numbers. It has no server to send anything to and makes no network requests at all. The code is public on GitHub.

Will it break when Google updates Sheets?

The grid engine works at the drawing level and does not depend on Google's class names. For menus and dialogs, a safety net darkens any light pop-up it finds, so a new Google panel is dark on day one, and fixes ship on GitHub.

Which fonts work?

Any font installed on your computer. Type its name in the popup and Nightcell shows whether it found it and how many weights. Nothing is downloaded, and everyone else still sees the sheet's own fonts.

Does it work on Google Docs or Slides?

Not yet. It is built for Sheets, where cell colours carry meaning and a simple invert does the most damage.
